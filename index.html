Ask. Argue. Convene. Bury.

HQ sits twice a day. Every session runs three rounds — blind proposals, critique and champions, final vote — and delivers two ideas with a full pitch deck. The founder holds the last word, always.

  • 01Ask the council — put an idea on the table and the bench takes positions blind, before anyone knows whose idea it was
  • 02Members argue their seat — the Critic hunts the flaw, the Engineer prices the build, Finance runs the money math; positions clash in a running debate stream
  • 03Convene for the verdict — votes land, the sharpest objection gets its answer on the record, and the delivered ideas go to a captioned pitch deck
  • 04Bury what dies, with honor — ideas the founder rejects after delivery rest in the graveyard, the session's sharpest objection as epitaph, on the do-not-repeat list forever

Seven seats. No yes-men.

A fixed bench of personas, each honest to its seat — the strange idea, the flaw, the build plan, the buyer, the market, the money, the law. When a seat likes something, it says exactly what would have to be true.

🎨

Creative

The idea machine who says the strange idea out loud — allergic to the obvious me-too. Carries the transplant lens: what worked at Uber, Airbnb, and Stripe, pitched where it hasn't landed yet.

🧐

Critic

Critiques everything and kills weak ideas early so they never get built. Sharp, unsentimental, allergic to hype — and precise about what would have to be true.

🛠️

Engineer

Judges what can actually ship, in how many weeks, on which model capabilities — and where the technical moat is. Turns concepts into plans and calls out fantasy.

🎯

Product

Embeds with people doing real jobs — recruiters, nurses, bookkeepers, dispatchers — and hunts hair-on-fire drudgery. Cares most about the first ten users and whether each tells a friend.

📣

Marketer

Owns the outside view: positioning, pricing, distribution. Who signs the check, what budget line the product replaces, which channel spreads it. Allergic to build-it-and-they-will-come.

💰

Finance

The money math: unit economics, gross margin, churn sensitivity, the path from first dollar to covering rent. Asks what the tenth customer pays and what the hundredth costs to acquire.

The seventh seat — 🛡️ Legal — reads every idea for the ways it breaks: another vendor's terms, sensitive data without a privacy story, licensed professions, and liability when the AI is wrong. Not legal advice, just the seat's honest read.

A paper trail, in plain files.

The whole council runs on plain markdown and JSON — readable, diffable, version-controlled. Every debate, verdict, pitch, and burial is a file in the repo, so the entire history of the council is reviewable commit by commit.

💬

debates.md

The durable transcript of every session and conference-room run — blind proposals, champions, votes, and the sharpest objections with their answers.

🎤

pitches.md

The Product seat's captioned decks: five slides per delivered idea — problem and buyer, first version, why now, money, and the sharpest objection answered.

🪦

graveyard.md

Where delivered-and-rejected ideas rest, with cause and epitaph. Buried ideas never return — they stay on the do-not-repeat list forever.

📅

calendar.md

The company calendar and standing rhythm: HQ sits at 08:00 and 20:00. Sessions, burials, and build kickoffs write themselves here.
